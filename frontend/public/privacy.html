HockeyQuant Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 6, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 6, 2026

HockeyQuant ("HockeyQuant," "we," "us," or "our") provides NHL analytics, game predictions, and informational sportsbook odds through our mobile app and website (together, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using the Service you agree to this Privacy Policy.

HockeyQuant is an informational and entertainment product. We do not accept wagers, process bets, or handle gambling funds. Any odds, lines, or "edges" shown are for information only. The Service is intended for adults (17+) and is not directed to children.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide. Account information (your email address and an encrypted password; or, if you use Sign in with Google, your name, email, and Google profile identifier — never your Google password); profile preferences (such as favorite team and display name); and user content (predictions/picks, custom models and their weightings, and related activity).

Information collected automatically. Usage and performance data (screens viewed, features used, error logs, and pick accuracy associated with your account) and technical data from our hosting providers (such as IP address, device/OS type, and timestamps) for security and reliability.

What we do not collect. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites (no App Tracking Transparency prompt). We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or health data, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to create and secure your account and authenticate sign-in (including Google sign-in); provide core features (predictions, daily picks, accuracy tracking, custom models, streaks/XP, leaderboards, achievements); operate, maintain, debug, and improve the Service; communicate about your account; and protect against fraud and abuse. We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. NHL data, statistics, and odds

The Service displays hockey schedules, statistics, projections, and sportsbook odds from third-party sources (including the NHL's public data, statistical providers, and licensed odds feeds). This sports data describes games and players, not you, and is provided for information only — it may be delayed or inaccurate. HockeyQuant is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the National Hockey League, any NHL team, or any sportsbook. To generate plain-language explanations of a prediction, we may send non-personal game context (such as team names and statistics) to an AI text-generation provider; we do not send your personal account information for this purpose.
