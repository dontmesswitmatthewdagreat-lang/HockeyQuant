# HockeyQuant Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** June 6, 2026
**Last updated:** June 6, 2026

HockeyQuant ("HockeyQuant," "we," "us," or "our") provides NHL analytics,
game predictions, and informational sportsbook odds through our mobile app and
website (together, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what
information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices
you have.

By using the Service you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree,
please do not use the Service.

> **HockeyQuant is an informational and entertainment product.** We do not
> accept wagers, process bets, or handle gambling funds. Any odds, lines, or
> "edges" shown are for information only. The Service is intended for adults
> (17+) and is not directed to children.

---

## 1. Information we collect

### a. Information you provide
- **Account information.** When you create an account, we collect your **email
  address** and an encrypted password. If you sign in with **Google**, we
  receive your name, email address, and Google profile identifier from Google
  (we never receive your Google password).
- **Profile preferences.** Optional details such as your favorite team and
  display name.
- **User content.** Predictions/picks you make, custom prediction models you
  create (including the weightings you choose), and related activity.

### b. Information collected automatically
- **Usage and performance data.** Basic interaction and diagnostic information
  (for example, screens viewed, features used, error logs, and accuracy of
  your picks) associated with your account so we can operate gameplay,
  leaderboards, and statistics.
- **Technical data.** Standard server logs from our hosting providers (such as
  IP address, device/OS type, and timestamps) used for security and
  reliability.

### c. Information we do **not** collect
- We do **not** use third-party advertising SDKs and we do **not** track you
  across other companies' apps or websites. We therefore do not present an App
  Tracking Transparency prompt.
- We do **not** collect precise location, contacts, photos, or health data.
- We do **not** knowingly collect information from children under 13.

---

## 2. How we use your information

We use the information above to:
- Create and secure your account and authenticate sign-in (including Google
  sign-in).
- Provide core features: game predictions, your daily picks, accuracy
  tracking, custom models, streaks/XP, leaderboards, and achievements.
- Operate, maintain, debug, and improve the Service.
- Communicate with you about your account (for example, email confirmation or
  security notices).
- Protect against fraud, abuse, and violations of our terms.

We do **not sell** your personal information, and we do not use it for
third-party advertising.

## 3. NHL data, statistics, and odds

The Service displays hockey schedules, statistics, projections, and sportsbook
odds obtained from third-party sources (including the NHL's public data,
statistical providers, and licensed odds feeds). This sports data is **not your
personal information** — it describes games and players, not you. Odds and
lines are provided for informational purposes and may be delayed or inaccurate.
HockeyQuant is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the National
Hockey League, any NHL team, or any sportsbook.

To generate plain-language explanations of a prediction, we may send
**non-personal game context** (such as team names and statistics) to an AI
text-generation provider. We do not send your personal account information for
this purpose.

## 4. How we share information

We share personal information only with **service providers (processors)** who
help us run the Service, and only as needed for them to perform their function:

| Provider | Purpose | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Authentication and database hosting | Account, profile, picks, models |
| **Google** | "Sign in with Google" authentication | Name, email, Google profile ID |
| **Render** | Backend/API hosting | Server logs, API request data |
| **Vercel** | Website hosting | Server logs (web) |
| **AI text provider** | Generating prediction summaries | Non-personal game context only |

We may also disclose information (a) to comply with law or valid legal process,
(b) to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the Service, or
(c) in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case
we will notify you of any change in how your information is handled.

## 5. Data retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as
needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account, we delete or

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Access and update.** You can view and update your profile in the app.
- **Account deletion.** You can delete your account and associated personal
  data from within the app (Profile → account settings) or by emailing us at
  the address below. Deleting your account removes your picks, models, and
  profile.
- **Regional rights.** Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA/UK
  under GDPR, or California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access,
  correct, delete, port, or restrict processing of your personal information,
  and to object to certain processing. We do not sell or "share" personal
  information as those terms are defined under California law. To exercise any
  right, contact us; we will not discriminate against you for doing so.
